Truth Tables

Build and verify truth tables for compound Boolean expressions, with instant feedback on every row.

Overview

  • Click Show Steps to reveal the truth table for each problem.
  • Fill in the output column(s) for each input combination.
  • Click Check Answers to get instant feedback. Correct answers are highlighted in green, mistakes in red.
  • Click Hide Steps to collapse the table and clear your work for that problem.
  • Tables with many columns/rows can be scrolled horizontally on small screens.
